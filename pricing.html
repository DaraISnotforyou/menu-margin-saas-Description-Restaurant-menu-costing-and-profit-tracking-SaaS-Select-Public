Questions

Can I subscribe today?

Not yet. This is a pricing preview while account security, cloud storage and payment handling are being built and tested.

Why offer yearly billing?

The proposed annual plan is $99 upfront instead of $120 over 12 monthly payments, a saving of $21.

Will my restaurant data be backed up?

Not in the current prototype. At present, demo data is stored on the device. Secure cloud backup is a planned Pro feature and must be implemented before the plan is sold.

How will payments work?

For a Nigerian launch, Paystack is a candidate for recurring payments. A secure backend and verified payment webhooks must be configured before subscriptions can activate.
